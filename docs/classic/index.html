come as you are . . .

You don’t need to have everything figured out before coming to therapy. We’ll begin exactly where you are, with the understanding that you are the expert on your own life. I work alongside you to uncover the roots of the emotions and patterns that have been causing discomfort, while helping you understand how the different pieces of your story may still be affecting you today.

If your own mind hasn’t felt like a safe place lately, we’ll begin by creating one together in therapy. Sharing your story can feel incredibly vulnerable, especially when you’re still discovering what certain experiences mean or how deeply they’ve shaped you. That’s where I come in: to help you stay grounded and moving forward. If you feel called to look beneath the surface and rediscover yourself, I’ve been told I’m pretty good company.

meet Paige

Greetings! I’m Paige Haile - a Florida Licensed Mental Health Counselor and the founder of Highly Aligned Therapy :) Based out of Tampa, I support clients navigating anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, and loss using therapy, mindfulness, body-based regulation, and Reiki-informed principles.

I blend evidence-based tools such as cognitive behavioral therapy with psychodynamic exploration, mindfulness, and genuine human connection. Together, we make space for both practical change and a deeper understanding of who you are. You don’t need to have everything figured out before you begin. You’re encouraged to come exactly as you are.

ABOUT paige

I became a therapist because I understand what it feels like to be trapped inside your own mind—navigating anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, and loss while wondering if you’re behind or if something is wrong with you. Through therapy, I found the clarity to move through the mental fog, better understand my experiences, and reconnect with my own strength. My continued growth, both personally and professionally, has only deepened my belief in the power of this work.

I believe that understanding your story allows you to reclaim your power, feel more confident in who you are, and create a life that feels fulfilling and aligned. The goal is not to erase what you have been through, but to help you stand firmly on your story rather than continuing to carry its weight.

I consider myself a lifelong student—always learning, growing, and remaining curious. This curiosity brings an open and thoughtful energy into our sessions. My training has taught me to approach each client with unconditional positive regard, creating a space rooted in compassion, acceptance, and freedom from judgment.
